15 SERVICES

Industrial and Commercial Services

In addition to its product portfolio, AKFA Aluminium JSC provides a range of industrial, commercial and support services related to construction, installation, customer communication and the development of partner business formats.

  • Construction and Installation Works Building, assembly and installation works for construction and engineering projects.
  • Royalty and IMZO Franchise Services Franchise-related services and royalty arrangements within the IMZO partner business model.
  • Anodizing Services Surface treatment services for aluminium profiles, providing enhanced durability, corrosion resistance and a refined decorative finish.
  • Elevator Equipment Installation Services Installation services related to elevator equipment and technical building systems.
