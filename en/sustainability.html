01 SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY

Responsible Industrial Growth

AKFA Aluminium approaches sustainability as an integral part of long-term industrial development. The Company’s sustainability strategy focuses on responsible production, efficient use of resources, development of human capital, community engagement and transparent corporate governance.

As an industrial group operating across key areas of the construction sector, AKFA Aluminium recognizes its responsibility to reduce environmental impact, strengthen operational discipline and support long-term value creation for customers, employees, shareholders, partners and society.

02 SUSTAINABILITY PRIORITIES

AKFA Aluminium’s sustainability approach is built around practical priorities that are directly connected with the Company’s industrial activity: resource efficiency, responsible production, people and communities, and governance transparency.

Resource Efficiency Using Resources with Discipline

The Company focuses on efficient use of materials, energy and production resources. This approach supports operational stability, reduces unnecessary losses and strengthens the long-term sustainability of industrial processes.

Responsible Production Industrial Development with Control

AKFA Aluminium develops production capabilities with attention to quality, technological discipline and operational responsibility. The Company’s manufacturing base supports construction, architecture and infrastructure projects while maintaining a focus on reliability and process control.

People and Communities Supporting Employees and Society

Sustainability for AKFA Aluminium includes the development of human capital, safe and respectful working conditions, internal engagement, community support and social initiatives that contribute to long-term trust with stakeholders.

Governance and Transparency Building a Transparent Corporate Framework

As a joint-stock company, AKFA Aluminium continues to develop corporate governance, reporting and disclosure practices. Transparency and accountability are important elements of the Company’s responsible growth model.
