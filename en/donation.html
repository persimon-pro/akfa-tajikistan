01 Blood Donation Initiatives

Supporting Healthcare Through Employee Participation

AKFA Aluminium supports blood donation initiatives as part of its social responsibility and community engagement approach. Employees of the Company regularly participate in donor campaigns, contributing to healthcare needs and helping strengthen a culture of mutual support. The Company actively cooperates with the Blood Transfusion Institute to organize donor activities in a responsible and coordinated format. These initiatives reflect AKFA Aluminium’s commitment to supporting society not only through industrial development, but also through practical social contribution.

Blood Donation Initiatives

AKFA Aluminium supports donor initiatives that unite employees around a meaningful social purpose and contribute to the wider healthcare system.

  • EMPLOYEE PARTICIPATION Regular Participation in Donor Campaigns

    AKFA Aluminium employees regularly take part in blood donation campaigns organized with the participation of healthcare specialists. These initiatives create an opportunity for employees to contribute to society through practical and meaningful action.

  • HEALTHCARE COOPERATION Cooperation with the Blood Transfusion Institute

    The Company actively cooperates with the Blood Transfusion Institute to support the proper organization of donor activities. This partnership helps ensure that blood donation campaigns are carried out in a coordinated, responsible and socially useful format.

  • SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY Supporting Communities Through Action

    Blood donation initiatives reflect AKFA Aluminium’s commitment to supporting communities beyond its production activities. Through employee participation, the Company contributes to healthcare needs and encourages a culture of care and solidarity.

  • CORPORATE VALUES Building a Culture of Care and Engagement

    Donor campaigns help strengthen the Company’s internal culture by bringing employees together around shared values. These initiatives support responsibility, compassion and active engagement as important elements of AKFA Aluminium’s corporate culture.
