01 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Executive Summary Ismail Israilov Member of the Supervisory Board, JSC AKFA ALUMINIUM
info@akfa-group.tj

More than 15 years of senior management experience in strategic management, commercial activities, manufacturing, and investment and construction projects. He has managed manufacturing and commercial businesses, including a company operating in the aluminium building materials sector, and has participated in the development of production capacity and new business lines.

Core Expertise

Strategic and Operational Management Business Development and Commercial Growth Investment and Construction Project Management Corporate Governance and Organizational Development Sales Development and Market Expansion Production and Commercial Operations Business Process Optimization Financial Performance Improvement Full-Cycle Project Management Strategic Partnerships and Stakeholder Relations Government, Investor and Partner Engagement Change Management and Management Team Development

Key functions and areas of responsibility

Strategic and operational management; business and commercial development; management of investment and construction projects; coordination of manufacturing, financial and commercial functions; development of sales and target markets; business process optimization; interaction with investors, government authorities and strategic partners.

Education

Tashkent Automobile and Road Institute

Bachelor’s degree in Motor Transport Management

Tashkent State University of Economics

Master’s degree in Industry Management
