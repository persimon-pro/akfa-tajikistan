01 COMPANY PROFILE

Building Materials and Engineering Solutions

AKFA Building Materials brings together manufacturing capabilities in building materials, heating systems, aluminium composite panels, accessories, coatings and industrial components. The company expands AKFA Aluminium’s production portfolio and supports the construction industry with materials and solutions for modern residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

  • 75,490 m² / Production facilities area
  • 2,500+ Employees at AKFA Building Materials
  • 30,000 Aluminium profiles production capacity (tones/year)
  • 4,000,000 Radiator production capacity (sections/year)
  • 450,000 AAC production capacity (m³/year)
  • 50,000,000 Accessories production capacity

02 COMPANY STRUCTURE

AKFA Building Materials combines several production areas that support the development of building materials, engineering products, accessories and industrial components for the construction industry.

  • CORPORATE CENTRE Management and Coordination

    The corporate centre coordinates production, commercial, administrative and operational activities across AKFA Building Materials. Its role is to ensure alignment between manufacturing units, supply chain processes and business priorities. This creates a unified management platform for the company’s diversified industrial operations.

  • ALUMINIUM PROFILES Aluminium Profile Production

    The aluminium profile production unit manufactures profile systems for construction, architectural and industrial applications. Its output supports a wide range of solutions, from building structures and interior systems to facade and engineering components. This production area forms one of the key foundations of AKFA Building Materials’ industrial portfolio.

  • HEATING SYSTEMS Heating Systems Production

    The heating systems production unit manufactures aluminium and bimetal radiators for residential, commercial and industrial buildings. These products are developed to support reliable heat distribution, durability and efficient performance in different operating conditions. The unit expands the company’s product portfolio within the building engineering segment.

  • Extrusion Dies Extrusion Dies Production

    The extrusion dies unit produces tooling components used in industrial extrusion processes. This capability supports manufacturing precision, process stability and the development of profile systems across the company’s production ecosystem. By maintaining in-house tooling expertise, AKFA Building Materials strengthens control over quality, efficiency and production flexibility.

  • AAC & FIBRECEMENT Autoclaved Aerated Concrete and Fibre Cement Production

    This production area focuses on autoclaved aerated concrete and fibre cement materials for modern construction. The products are designed to support durable, energy-efficient and practical building solutions across residential, commercial and infrastructure projects. Together, these materials strengthen the company’s position in the building products segment.

  • Aluminium Composite Panels Aluminium Composite Panels Production

    The aluminium composite panels unit produces materials used in facade, architectural and exterior finishing solutions. These products support modern building design by combining visual flexibility, functional performance and industrial consistency. The production line contributes to the company’s ability to serve projects requiring reliable and visually refined cladding materials.

  • W&D ACCESSORIES Window and Door Accessories Production

    The window and door accessories unit manufactures components used in window, door and transparent structure systems. These products support the performance, durability and functional reliability of finished construction solutions. The unit strengthens vertical integration within the Group’s broader building products ecosystem.

  • SEALS AND POLYMER COATINGS Seals and Polymer Coatings Production

    This production area manufactures EPDM seals, polymer-based coatings and related industrial materials. These components play an important role in insulation, sealing, surface protection and product performance. The unit supports both internal manufacturing needs and broader applications across construction and industrial segments.

  • ROAD, EPOXY AND FIRE-RESISTANT COATINGS Road, Epoxy and Fire-Resistant Coatings Production

    The specialized coatings unit produces road marking materials, epoxy coatings and fire-resistant coating solutions. These products are used in construction, infrastructure and industrial applications where durability, protection and functional performance are essential. The unit expands AKFA Building Materials’ presence beyond core construction materials into applied industrial solutions.

  • Logistics Infrastructure Regional Warehouse Management

    Regional warehouse facilities support the storage, handling and availability of AKFA Building Materials products across key markets. This infrastructure helps maintain supply continuity, improve delivery efficiency and support customer demand across different product categories. It plays an important role in connecting production capacity with market distribution.

  • Logistics Infrastructure Freight Logistics Management

    The freight logistics function coordinates the transportation and delivery of products across domestic and export markets. Its role is to ensure efficient movement of materials from production sites to warehouses, partners and project destinations. This supports operational reliability and strengthens the company’s supply chain performance.
