AKFA Aluminium Participates in UzBuild 2026 International Construction Exhibition

AKFA Aluminium took part in UzBuild 2026, one of the key international exhibitions for the construction and building materials industry, presenting its industrial capabilities, product portfolio and solutions for modern architecture and infrastructure.

AKFA Aluminium JSC participated in the UzBuild 2026 International Construction Exhibition, a major industry platform bringing together manufacturers, developers, architects, engineers and business representatives from across the construction sector.

At the exhibition, the Company presented a broad range of products and solutions reflecting its integrated industrial profile. The exposition showcased AKFA Aluminium’s capabilities in aluminium and PVC profile systems, façade and glazing solutions, heating systems, building materials and related industrial products for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

Participation in UzBuild 2026 provided an opportunity to strengthen dialogue with professional audiences, demonstrate the scale of the Company’s manufacturing base and highlight AKFA Aluminium’s role in supporting the development of Uzbekistan’s construction industry through high-quality locally based production.

The exhibition also served as an important platform for expanding business connections, presenting new directions of development and promoting the Company’s approach to industrial growth, product quality and long-term partnership.

Industry Platform

UzBuild 2026 became an effective platform for AKFA Aluminium to present its manufacturing potential, strengthen professional engagement and demonstrate solutions for modern construction and architecture.

AKFA Aluminium continues to participate in key industry events that contribute to market visibility, stakeholder engagement and the promotion of advanced industrial solutions. Through such platforms, the Company reinforces its position as one of Uzbekistan’s major industrial players in construction-related manufacturing.
