AKFA Aluminium at the Uzbekistan–Iraq Business Forum

AKFA Aluminium took part in the Uzbekistan–Iraq Business Forum, presenting the Company’s industrial capabilities, export potential and role in the development of modern construction-related manufacturing.

AKFA Aluminium participated in the Uzbekistan–Iraq Business Forum, a business platform focused on strengthening economic cooperation, industrial dialogue and partnership opportunities between companies and institutions of the two countries.

During the forum, AKFA Aluminium presented its manufacturing capabilities and product portfolio across key areas of the construction industry. The Company’s activities include aluminium and PVC profile systems, glazed and façade structures, heating systems, building materials and other solutions used in residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

Participation in the forum provided an opportunity to introduce AKFA Aluminium’s industrial scale, technological development and export-oriented approach to a wider international business audience. The event also supported dialogue with potential partners interested in construction materials, industrial cooperation and long-term supply relationships.

AKFA Aluminium continues to develop its international presence and strengthen cooperation with foreign markets. The Company views business forums and international platforms as important tools for building partnerships, presenting Uzbekistan’s industrial potential and supporting the expansion of local manufacturing beyond the domestic market.

International Cooperation

The Uzbekistan–Iraq Business Forum became an important platform for AKFA Aluminium to present its industrial expertise, strengthen business dialogue and explore new opportunities for international partnership.

AKFA Aluminium remains focused on sustainable industrial growth, product quality and the development of reliable partnerships. Through participation in international business events, the Company continues to promote its capabilities and contribute to the growth of Uzbekistan’s export-oriented industrial sector.
