01 GLOBAL PRESENCE

Expanding Industrial Reach Beyond Uzbekistan

AKFA Aluminium JSC develops its international presence through export operations, long-term partnerships and the supply of industrial products to markets across Central Asia, Europe, the Middle East, North America and other regions.

The Company’s global activity is supported by its integrated manufacturing base in Uzbekistan, diversified product portfolio and cooperation with international partners across construction, infrastructure and industrial sectors.
