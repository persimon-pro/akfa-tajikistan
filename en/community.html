01 Community Support

Care, Protection and Community Support

Mehr Shelter is a social responsibility initiative focused on supporting homeless animals and promoting responsible community values. The project reflects AKFA Aluminium’s commitment to social engagement beyond industrial operations. Through support for Mehr Shelter, the Company contributes to care infrastructure, animal welfare and public awareness around responsible treatment of animals.

  • 1,000+ Shelter capacity
  • 1 ha Dedicated shelter area
  • 5 Years of care
  • 50+ Support activities

COMMUNITY SUPPORT

AKFA Aluminium supports Mehr Shelter as part of its social responsibility efforts, contributing to animal welfare, community support and the promotion of a more humane approach to care and protection.

  • COMMUNITY SUPPORT A Social Responsibility Project

    Mehr Shelter represents a long-term community-oriented project focused on care, protection and responsible treatment of homeless animals. AKFA Aluminium supports the shelter as part of its broader commitment to social responsibility and public wellbeing.

  • ANIMAL WELFARE Care and Protection

    The shelter provides dedicated infrastructure for the accommodation, care and protection of animals. Support for Mehr Shelter helps create safer conditions for homeless dogs and contributes to a more humane approach to animal welfare.

  • DEDICATED INFRASTRUCTURE Facilities for Long-Term Care

    Mehr Shelter includes infrastructure designed to support daily care, veterinary assistance, accommodation and future adoption-related activities. The project demonstrates how corporate resources can be directed toward meaningful social impact.

  • PUBLIC AWARENESS Promoting Responsible Attitudes

    Beyond direct support, the project helps raise awareness about responsible treatment of animals and the importance of community participation. AKFA Aluminium sees such initiatives as part of a wider culture of responsibility, care and engagement.
