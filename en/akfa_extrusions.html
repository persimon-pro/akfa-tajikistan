01 COMPANY PROFILE

Profile Systems and Industrial Components

AKFA Extrusions brings together manufacturing capabilities in aluminium and PVC profile systems, polymer materials, sealing components and industrial products used in modern window, door and facade solutions. The company forms one of the core production pillars of AKFA Aluminium’s industrial portfolio.

  • 203,300 m² / Production facilities area
  • 2,050+ Employees at AKFA Extrusions
  • 50,000 Annual aluminium profile capacity, tonnes
  • 60,000 Annual PVC profile capacity, tonnes

02 MANUFACTURING STRUCTURE

AKFA Extrusions combines several production areas that support the development of profile systems, components and materials for the construction industry.

  • ALUMINIUM PROFILES Aluminium Profile Production

    The aluminium profile production unit manufactures profile systems for window, door, facade and architectural applications. Its products support a wide range of construction and engineering solutions, combining industrial precision with functional performance. This direction forms one of the core manufacturing capabilities within AKFA EXTRUSIONS.

  • PVC PROFILES PVC Profile Production

    The PVC profile production unit focuses on systems used in modern window and door structures. These profiles are developed for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects where reliability, insulation performance and consistent product quality are essential. The direction strengthens AKFA EXTRUSIONS’ position as a key supplier of profile systems for the construction market.

  • POLYMER MATERIALS PVC Granules and Sealing Components

    This production area covers polymer materials, PVC granules and sealing components used in profile, window and glazing systems. These materials support product durability, insulation performance and the stable operation of finished construction solutions. The unit contributes to greater vertical integration within the Group’s manufacturing ecosystem.

  • PROTECTIVE MATERIALS Protective Materials Production

    The protective materials unit manufactures materials used for product protection, packaging and industrial handling processes. These solutions help preserve product quality during storage, transportation and installation. The direction supports operational efficiency across the company’s wider production and supply chain activities.

  • COMPONENTS Spacer Bars and Metal Reinforcement

    The components production unit manufactures spacer bars, metal reinforcement and related elements used in insulating glass units, window systems and construction solutions. These components play an important role in structural stability, system performance and long-term reliability. The direction strengthens AKFA Extrusions’ ability to supply not only profiles, but also critical supporting components for finished systems.
