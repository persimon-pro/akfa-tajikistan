01 WHY AKFA ALUMINIUM?

AKFA Aluminium — An Integrated Industrial Group from Uzbekistan

AKFA Aluminium JSC is an integrated industrial group with a large-scale manufacturing base, a diversified product portfolio and a growing international presence. The Company operates across key areas of the construction industry, producing profile systems, glazed and façade structures, heating systems and building materials. As a joint-stock company, AKFA Aluminium is developing a transparent corporate framework based on governance, reporting and disclosure practices. This Investor Relations section is designed to provide shareholders, investors and financial stakeholders with accurate, relevant and up-to-date information about the Company’s development, corporate governance and official disclosures.

02 INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS

Industrial Scale, Diversification and Governance Development

AKFA Aluminium’s investment profile is based on its integrated manufacturing platform, broad exposure to the construction industry, export potential and ongoing development of corporate governance and disclosure practices.

01 Integrated Manufacturing Platform

The Company combines several industrial capabilities within one group, supporting production efficiency, operational control and product diversification.

02 Diversified Product Portfolio

AKFA Aluminium serves multiple product areas, including profile systems, façade and glazed structures, heating systems and building materials.

03 Export-Oriented Development

The Company’s international activity supports broader market access and strengthens its role as an industrial exporter from Uzbekistan.

04 Institutional Transformation

The transition to a joint-stock company creates a stronger basis for governance, reporting, transparency and investor communication.

05 Strategic Role in Industry

AKFA Aluminium contributes to Uzbekistan’s construction materials industry and supports modern architecture, infrastructure and urban development.

06 Disclosure and IR Development

The Investor Relations section is designed to provide structured access to official information, financial materials and corporate disclosures.
