01 CORPORATE PROFILE

AKFA Aluminium — An Integrated Industrial Group from Uzbekistan

AKFA Aluminium is an integrated industrial group operating across key areas of the construction industry. The company brings together manufacturing capabilities in profile systems, glazed and façade structures, heating systems and building materials, supporting modern architecture, construction and urban infrastructure.

  • 10 Manufacturing Facilities
  • 439,000 m² / Manufacturing Area
  • 5,750+ Employees
  • 30+ Export Markets

02 MISSION, VISION AND VALUES

  • AKFA Aluminium Mission

    To strengthen Uzbekistan’s industrial potential by producing advanced solutions for modern construction, developing professional expertise, and expanding the presence of locally manufactured products in international markets.

  • AKFA Aluminium Vision

    To become an internationally recognized industrial group in the construction sector, representing Uzbekistan through manufacturing scale, product quality, technological development, and a mature corporate model.

  • AKFA Aluminium Values

    Our values reflect the way AKFA Aluminium develops as an industrial group: commitment to quality, technological progress, responsible growth, and long-term trust with customers, partners, shareholders and communities.

03 FOUNDER’S MESSAGE

  • FOUNDER’S MESSAGE We don't simply manufacture products — We build the infrastructure of people's lives

    Quality has never been a goal for us. It has always been the only option. Every product that carries the AKFA name carries our word — and we have never broken it.

    AKFA Aluminium was founded on a simple belief: that Uzbekistan deserves world-class industry, built by its own people, for its own future. Twenty six years on, that belief has never been stronger — and neither have we.
