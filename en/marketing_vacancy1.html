Digital Marketing Specialist

  • HOME/
  • CAREERS/
  • MARKETING DEPARTMENT/
  • Digital Marketing Specialist
FULL TIME WORK Digital Marketing Specialist
Requirements
  • Higher education in marketing, PR, advertising, economics or a related field
  • At least 2 years of experience in digital marketing
  • Understanding of performance marketing, SMM, targeted and contextual advertising
  • Experience with Meta Ads, Google Ads, Telegram Ads and analytics tools
  • Ability to analyze the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and prepare reports
  • Understanding of digital funnels, lead generation and online brand promotion
  • Experience in preparing briefs for designers, copywriters, video editors and contractors
  • Strong written communication skills in Russian; knowledge of Uzbek and English will be an advantage
Responsibilities
  • Planning and launching digital campaigns to promote the Company’s brands and products
  • Managing advertising campaigns across social media, search engines and digital channels
  • Analyzing the effectiveness of advertising placements and preparing optimization recommendations
  • Participating in the development of content plans, digital activations and special projects
  • Coordinating work with designers, SMM specialists, contractors and media partners
  • Reviewing visual and text materials before publication
  • Monitoring digital trends, competitor activity and new promotion tools
  • Supporting the development of the Company’s corporate digital platforms
Benefits
  • Stable and engaging work with opportunities for professional growth
  • Paid vacation and sick leave
  • Lunch provided at the Company’s expense
  • Official employment in accordance with the Labor Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan
  • Competitive salary and benefits
  • Work schedule: 6/1, from 9:00 to 18:00
  • Comfortable office with all necessary conditions
