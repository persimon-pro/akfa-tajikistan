01 Environmental Initiatives

Environmental Awareness and Action

AKFA Aluminium views environmental responsibility as an important part of long-term industrial development. The Company supports initiatives focused on greening, cleanup activities, employee participation and community engagement. Through environmental actions, AKFA Aluminium contributes to the development of a more responsible corporate culture and supports practical steps aimed at improving the surrounding environment.

  • 70,000 Trees planted
  • 1,000+ Employees involved
  • 20+ Environmental activities
  • 10+ Locations supported

Environmental Initiatives

AKFA Aluminium supports environmental initiatives aimed at promoting responsible engagement with nature, improving urban green spaces and strengthening environmental awareness among employees and communities.

  • ENVIRONMENTAL ACTION Tree Planting and Greening Initiatives

    AKFA Aluminium supports greening initiatives aimed at improving urban spaces and strengthening environmental awareness. Tree planting activities help create a more sustainable urban environment and encourage employees to participate in practical ecological actions.

  • EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT Building a Culture of Responsibility

    Environmental initiatives are also a way to engage employees in shared values beyond the production process. Participation in ecological activities helps strengthen teamwork, responsibility and awareness of the Company’s role in society.

  • COMMUNITY AND ENVIRONMENT Supporting Local Communities

    AKFA Aluminium’s environmental activities are focused not only on internal corporate responsibility, but also on supporting communities. Cleanup campaigns, greening initiatives and awareness-based actions contribute to a more responsible approach to the environment.

  • LONG-TERM APPROACH Environmental Responsibility in Industrial Development

    As an industrial group, AKFA Aluminium recognizes the importance of reducing environmental impact and supporting responsible growth. Environmental initiatives form part of the Company’s broader sustainability approach, alongside efficient resource use, production discipline and corporate transparency.
