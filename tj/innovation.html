Инноватсияҳо

Innovation as an Industrial Capability

AKFA Aluminium approaches innovation as a practical industrial capability focused on product development, engineering, testing and quality control. The Company develops and improves profile systems, components and construction solutions to meet the technical requirements of modern architecture, construction and industrial customers. Innovation at AKFA Aluminium is supported by R&D activities, quality laboratories, production control and the application of technical standards. This approach helps the Company strengthen product reliability, improve manufacturing discipline and support the development of new solutions across its industrial portfolio.

INNOVATION AND QUALITY

AKFA Aluminium’s innovation activities are focused on product development, engineering expertise, laboratory testing and quality control. This approach supports the creation of reliable profile systems, components and construction solutions for modern architecture and industrial applications.

  • INNOVATIONS Product Development and Improvement

    AKFA Aluminium develops and improves products across its key business areas, including aluminium and PVC profile systems, components, glazed structures and building materials. The Company works on product configurations, system compatibility, material performance and production efficiency to respond to market needs and technical requirements. This continuous improvement process supports the creation of reliable, functional and competitive solutions for the construction industry.

  • RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT Engineering New Profile Systems

    The Company’s R&D activities support the development of new aluminium and PVC profile systems for window, door and façade applications. This includes product design, technical adaptation, performance improvement and preparation of solutions for serial production. R&D work helps AKFA Aluminium respond to the needs of architects, builders, industrial customers and international markets by developing products that combine functionality, quality and production scalability.

  • TECHNOLOGY AND QUALITY Manufacturing Discipline and Product Reliability

    Technology and quality are central to AKFA Aluminium’s industrial model. The Company applies production control processes designed to support stable product characteristics, consistent manufacturing quality and reliable performance across different product categories. This approach strengthens operational discipline and helps ensure that products are suitable for use in modern construction, façade engineering and infrastructure projects.

  • QUALITY LABORATORY Testing and Quality Control

    AKFA Aluminium operates quality control processes that support the testing of materials, components and finished products. Laboratory checks help maintain product consistency, technical accuracy and reliability across the Company’s manufacturing operations. The work of quality laboratories supports production decisions, product improvement and compliance with internal technical requirements.

  • MATERIAL AND SYSTEM TESTING Performance Verification

    The Company conducts testing of profile systems, components and construction solutions to verify their performance, durability and suitability for modern window, door and façade applications. Material and system testing helps AKFA Aluminium assess product behaviour under technical and operational requirements, supporting safer and more reliable use of products in real construction projects.
