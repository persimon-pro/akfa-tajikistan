01 COMPANY PROFILE

Конструксияҳои шишагӣ ва системаҳои фасадӣ

AKFA Assembly specializes in the production of finished glazed structures, window and door solutions, facade systems and insulating glass units. The company supports AKFA Aluminium’s industrial portfolio by transforming profile systems and components into ready-to-use construction solutions for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

  • 160,000 m² / Production facility area
  • 1,000+ Employees at AKFA Assembly
  • 14 Automated production lines
  • 750,000 Glazed structures capacity (m²/year)

02 MANUFACTURING STRUCTURE

AKFA Assembly combines manufacturing facilities for glazed structures and insulating glass units, supporting the production of finished construction solutions for projects of different scale and complexity.

  • IMZO TASHKENT Glazed Structures Production — Tashkent

    The IMZO Tashkent production facility manufactures finished window, door and glazed construction solutions for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects. Its operations combine fabrication, assembly and quality control processes to support reliable project execution. The facility plays a central role in AKFA Assembly’s production platform and serves as a key manufacturing hub for finished transparent structures.

  • IMZO NUKUS Glazed Structures Production — Nukus

    The IMZO Nukus production facility expands AKFA Assembly’s regional manufacturing capabilities and supports the production of glazed structures outside the capital. Its role is to improve product availability, strengthen regional supply and serve construction projects with finished window, door and transparent structure solutions. This facility contributes to a more flexible and geographically balanced production network.

  • INSULATING GLASS UNITS Insulating Glass Unit Production

    The insulating glass unit production line manufactures glass units used in window, door and facade systems. These components are essential for thermal insulation, acoustic performance, energy efficiency and the overall reliability of finished glazed structures. This capability strengthens AKFA Assembly’s vertical integration and supports consistent quality across the company’s product portfolio.
