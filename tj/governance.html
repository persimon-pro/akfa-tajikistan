  • Governance
  • GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK Strategic Oversight and Accountability

    AKFA Aluminium’s governance framework is built to support strategic supervision, responsible decision-making and long-term corporate development across the Group. The structure defines the roles of supervisory and executive bodies, ensuring accountability, transparency and alignment with the company’s business priorities. This approach strengthens institutional governance and supports the company’s continued growth as a major industrial group.

  • EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT Operational Leadership and Control

    The executive management team is responsible for implementing approved strategic decisions and coordinating the company’s key business functions. Its role includes operational control, performance management and the effective execution of industrial, commercial and administrative processes. This ensures that the company’s daily activities remain aligned with its long-term development objectives.

  • DISCLOSURE AND DOCUMENTS Transparency and Corporate Disclosure

    AKFA Aluminium provides access to corporate documents, governance information and official disclosures to support transparency for stakeholders, partners and investors. This section brings together key materials related to the company’s legal status, governance structure and corporate decision-making. The availability of such information reflects the company’s commitment to open and responsible corporate communication.
